# Interior science and diagram choices

Reviewed against online sources on **3 October 2026**. Covers the Sun, all eight planets, and the existing Moon and Pluto views. This is a simplified educational model, not a unique inversion of observational data.

## Reading the diagrams

The surviving hemisphere retains the surface texture. The sealed central cut face uses concentric regions at the model’s radial scale. Radii are fractions of the body’s reference radius, **not mass or volume fractions**. A thin crust stays thin; selecting it emphasizes its outline rather than inflating its thickness.

Solid rock/metal and ice have static textures. Fluid/plasma patterns are symbolic, with no physical timescale; reduced-motion settings freeze them. Colors distinguish materials and are not a temperature scale or the colors one would photograph inside a planet. Soft edges signify gradual transitions; hatching identifies a proposed region. A dashed interface marks a sharp boundary belonging to a hypothetical region, not a confidence interval.

“**Well constrained**” describes the major structure, not every chemical detail. “**Inferred**” describes a region supported by indirect observations or modeling. “**Possible**” identifies a region whose existence or separate identity remains unsettled. Uncertain radii are called illustrative or model dependent even where a region’s existence is supported.

The spherical views omit oblateness, lateral variation, small atmospheric layers, and many mineral phase changes. No single layer model can represent every currently allowed interior.

## Why Mars has separate scenarios

The 2025 study reports an inner-core detection, but the May 2026 review still lists its existence as an open question and notes unresolved compatibility with basal mantle melting. The interface/radius choices from these interpretations must not be combined into a supposedly established five-layer interior. The selector compares simplified interpretations; it does not imply they exhaust all permissible models or are necessarily mutually exclusive in reality.

## Visual improvements

- A wider initial view of the cut face exposes the interiors while retaining the curved textured exterior.
- Material-specific shader patterns replace the same grain/strata texture across every body.
- Selection strongly emphasizes the chosen region and places one matching numbered callout on the diagram.
- Clicking or tapping a visible cut-face region selects it; the list remains usable for thin crusts and keyboard access.
- Each region has a physical-state label, an evidence label, and an extent with its appropriate qualification.
- The Moon can display five layers; shader capacity is six, with no dropped innermost region.
- The mobile information card scrolls within a bounded height to keep the globe visible.

The reviewed sources and explanatory text are stored locally. They are a dated review, not a live scientific feed.
