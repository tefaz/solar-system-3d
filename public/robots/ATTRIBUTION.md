# Mars robot images

Official mission photographs and stitched self-portraits. Locally resized and encoded as WebP for the pop-ups; no generated imagery. Original compositions and colors are preserved. Spirit uses a polar panorama projection; Curiosity, Perseverance, InSight and Opportunity use stitched self-portraits.

- **Perseverance** (perseverance.webp): Perseverance’s self-portrait on Mars, with Ingenuity nearby. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS. [Original image and caption](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/perseverances-selfie-with-ingenuity/).
- **Curiosity** (curiosity.webp): Curiosity’s self-portrait on the rocky surface of Mars. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS. [Original image and caption](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/high-resolution-self-portrait-by-curiosity-rover-arm-camera/).
- **Zhurong** (zhurong.webp): Zhurong and its landing platform on Mars. Credit: CNSA. [Original image and caption](https://www.cnsa.gov.cn/english/n6465652/n6465653/c6840321/content.html).
- **Ingenuity** (ingenuity.webp): Ingenuity on Mars with its rotor blades released. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech. [Original image and caption](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/ingenuitys-blades-are-released/).
- **InSight** (insight.webp): InSight’s first self-portrait, showing its solar panels and instrument deck. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech. [Original image and caption](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/insights-first-selfie/).
- **Opportunity** (opportunity.webp): Opportunity’s self-portrait after wind cleaned its solar panels. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/Cornell/ASU. [Original image and caption](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/self-portrait-by-freshly-cleaned-opportunity-mars-rover-in-march-2014/).
- **Spirit** (spirit.webp): Spirit’s self-portrait in a polar projection of its McMurdo panorama. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/Cornell/ASU. [Original image and caption](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/spirit-mars-rover-in-mcmurdo-panorama-polar-projection/).
