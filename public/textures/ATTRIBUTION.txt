The maps are NASA-derived reconstructions using elevation and imagery data,
with colors adjusted using spacecraft observations. Earth uses NASA's Blue
Marble imagery. Some mapping gaps are reconstructed, and colors are enhanced.
Gas and ice giant textures depict cloud tops rather than a solid surface.

Downloaded maps: Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Earth day map and clouds, Mars,
Jupiter, Saturn and its ring alpha map, Uranus, Neptune, Moon, and Sun.
The Sun map is animated and color-treated by Orrery. Planet maps are unmodified.
Saturn's ring map is remapped onto radial UV coordinates.

Pluto: NASA New Horizons global color mosaic (2017), resized to 2048 x 1024.
Credit: NASA/JHUAPL/SwRI.
Source: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/pluto-global-color-map/
Asset: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/dynamicimage/assets/science/psd/solar/2023/09/p/l/pluto_color_mapmosaic.jpg
The original map has gaps in southern-hemisphere coverage. Black gap pixels
are rendered as plain neutral gray; no surface details are invented for them.
Resolution varies across the mosaic. Map colors are those of the source mosaic.
