# Natural night sky

Created with the built-in imagegen tool as a new replacement raster asset for Orrery.
File: `natural-sky.png`.

## Full generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: panoramic background texture for a browser solar system viewer. Create a 2:1 wide landscape image of a realistic dark, pristine night sky as photographed from Earth at a remote dark-sky location, without any ground or horizon. Aim for maximum available resolution and exceptionally clean fine detail. Predominantly deep neutral black, with tiny sharp natural stars of varied brightness, most extremely faint, and just a delicate broad Milky Way band crossing diagonally. Milky Way appears as faint smoky silver-gray starlight with subtle charcoal dust lanes, not dramatic bright clouds. Restrained neutral white and very faint warm ivory star colors. Keep the diffuse galactic light low contrast and understated; over 80 percent of the image should feel nearly black. A believable short-exposure astronomical photograph rather than colorful long-exposure astrophotography or science-fiction art. Quiet elegance, natural density, slight variation in star distribution, dark negative space throughout so planets remain prominent. No visible colored nebulae, no saturated purple, pink, blue, or teal, no glowing cloud masses, no bright galactic core, no galaxies seen from outside, no planets, no Sun, no comets, no star trails, no diffraction spikes, no lens flares, no terrain, no text, no logo, no watermark. Avoid oversized blurry stars and avoid an artificial uniform star grid. Left and right edges should connect naturally if repeated horizontally. The app will add a sparse layer of crisp stars, so the texture should not be crowded with bright foreground stars. Save the image and report its local file path for project integration.
